# 🧠 WorkMind AI

### AI-Powered Workforce Intelligence Platform

> Transform workforce data into intelligent HR decisions.

WorkMind AI is an AI-driven workforce management platform designed to help HR teams understand employee data, automate HR workflows, identify workforce risks, and make data-driven decisions across the employee lifecycle.

Unlike a traditional HR chatbot, WorkMind AI connects and reasons over multiple HR data sources to generate **explainable insights and actionable recommendations**.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Modern HR teams deal with large amounts of fragmented workforce data across:

- Recruitment
- Employee records
- Attendance
- Performance
- Engagement
- Skills
- HR policies

This makes it difficult to identify workforce risks and make timely, data-driven decisions.

**WorkMind AI addresses this challenge by bringing workforce intelligence into one platform.**

---

## 💡 What WorkMind AI Does

WorkMind AI provides an intelligent HR ecosystem that can:

- 🤖 Analyze and rank candidates
- 👋 Personalize employee onboarding
- 📚 Answer complex HR policy questions
- ⚠️ Identify employee attrition risk signals
- 📈 Analyze employee performance
- 🧩 Identify workforce skill gaps
- 🎤 Assist with AI-powered interviews
- 📊 Generate workforce-level insights
- ⚡ Recommend actionable HR interventions

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI Recruitment Intelligence

Analyze candidates based on:

- Skills
- Experience
- Education
- Job requirements
- Skill gaps

WorkMind AI provides an explainable candidate analysis instead of simply generating an unexplained score.

---

### 👋 Adaptive Onboarding Agent

Create personalized onboarding journeys based on:

- Employee role
- Department
- Skills
- Experience
- Required training

Example:

> WorkMind AI recommends completing cloud security training before assigning a new Software Engineer to a production project.

---

### 📚 HR Policy Intelligence

Ask complex HR policy questions and receive:

- Contextual answers
- Policy reasoning
- Relevant policy sources
- Confidence indicators
- Related policies

Example:

> "Can an employee carry forward unused leave after changing departments?"

---

### ⚠️ Attrition Intelligence

Identify employees showing potential attrition risk signals using workforce patterns such as:

- Engagement trends
- Absenteeism
- Workload
- Career growth
- Performance patterns

The system provides the evidence behind each risk signal and suggests possible HR actions.

> **Note:** Attrition predictions are presented as AI-generated risk signals, not certain outcomes.

---

### 📈 Performance Intelligence

Analyze:

- Goals
- Achievements
- Manager feedback
- Peer feedback
- Performance history

WorkMind AI can identify strengths and improvement areas and suggest development actions.
